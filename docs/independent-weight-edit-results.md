The upstream logs had 34 forks. These counts overlap, include news feeds and scaffolds, and cannot estimate the number of people experimenting. The examples below come from commit-pinned reports and the released model card. Their numbers are the respective teams’ measurements.

## New reports checked at 04:36 UTC

A later exact-phrase GitHub search returned 47 repositories. Upstream still had 34 forks. Several search hits were feeds or harnesses. People, completed runs, and independent discoveries cannot be counted from these two numbers.

- **Independent internal edit on Qwen.** Given the published layers, formula and strength, an ordinary numerical tool selected its own direction and columns from practice activations. On 11 of 12 other looping answers the edit reduced the probability of loop text more than that of earlier reasoning. Its mean selectivity was −0.033 nats/token versus −0.042 for the published edit and approximately zero for a norm-matched random edit. The outcome measures probabilities of supplied text. Whether this new edit gets more generated answers correct is open. [Pinned report](https://github.com/adiant211/wspacetest/blob/e92c221af2ad5bdeac29320f95aae68eda16e820/README.md)
- **Direct policy edit with fresh-seed improvement.** Gradless changed 180 weights so a small Breakout controller could anticipate the ball's next position. On 100 new paired seeds, mean reward rose from 0.80 to 4.03. The reported 95% bootstrap interval for the gain was 2.51 to 4.00. The team designed the starting controller. This tests one game and one seed distribution. [Results and exact edit](https://github.com/PDiTO/gradless/blob/19d8627bd17c5148989473f47c7688ce7b78aadb/README.md)
- **A lower math score after self-editing.** Qwen3.5-0.8B chose its own low-rank edit recipe from practice failures. With thinking off, the base answered 9/16 fresh math questions correctly and the edit answered 7/16. Both lost answers hit the generation cap. [Paired report](https://github.com/Schofi/weight-update-programming/blob/5be51586bcec41b0d9a8c4d22cbf304e9dd74c7a/results/math-direct-1024/report.json)
